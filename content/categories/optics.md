# Optics

Optics explores the nature of light, how it behaves, and how we see. This category covers reflection, refraction, interference, diffraction, and the properties of light.

## What is Optics?

Optics is the branch of physics that studies the behavior and properties of light, including its interactions with matter and the construction of instruments that use or detect it.

## Topics Covered

- Reflection and mirrors
- Refraction and lenses
- Wave properties of light
- Color and the visible spectrum
- Optical instruments
- Lasers and modern optics
