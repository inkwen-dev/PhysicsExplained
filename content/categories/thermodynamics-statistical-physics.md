# Thermodynamics & Statistical Physics

Thermodynamics & Statistical Physics explores heat, temperature, energy transfer, and the statistical behavior of large systems. This category covers the laws of thermodynamics, phase changes, and statistical mechanics.

## What is Thermodynamics?

Thermodynamics is the branch of physics that deals with heat, temperature, and energy and how they relate to work and other forms of energy. Statistical physics explains macroscopic properties in terms of microscopic particles.

## Topics Covered

- Heat and temperature
- Laws of thermodynamics
- Phase changes and states of matter
- Entropy and energy
- Statistical mechanics
- Heat engines and efficiency
