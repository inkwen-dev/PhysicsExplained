# Everyday Physics

Everyday Physics explores the physics principles behind common phenomena we encounter in daily life. This category answers questions about why things happen the way they do in our everyday world.

## What is Everyday Physics?

Everyday physics connects fundamental physics principles to the world around us, explaining common phenomena in terms of basic physical laws and concepts.

## Topics Covered

- Why objects fall
- Why ice floats
- Why the sky is blue
- How magnets work
- Everyday mechanical phenomena
- Common misconceptions
