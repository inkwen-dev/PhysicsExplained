# Electromagnetism

Electromagnetism explores electric and magnetic fields, electric currents, circuits, and electromagnetic waves. This category covers everything from static electricity to radio waves and light.

## What is Electromagnetism?

Electromagnetism is the branch of physics that studies the interactions between electric charges and magnetic fields. It unifies electricity and magnetism into a single theory.

## Topics Covered

- Static electricity and electric fields
- Electric current and circuits
- Magnetic fields and forces
- Electromagnetic induction
- Electromagnetic waves
- Applications in technology
