# Modern Physics

Modern Physics explores the revolutionary theories of the 20th century, including relativity and quantum mechanics. This category covers the strange and fascinating world of atoms, particles, and spacetime.

## What is Modern Physics?

Modern physics refers to physics developed in the 20th century and beyond, including Einstein's theory of relativity, quantum mechanics, particle physics, and nuclear physics.

## Topics Covered

- Special and general relativity
- Quantum mechanics
- Particle physics
- Nuclear physics
- The Standard Model
- Modern applications
