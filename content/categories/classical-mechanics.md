# Classical Mechanics

Classical Mechanics explores the motion of objects and the forces that cause motion. This category covers kinematics, dynamics, gravity, vibrations, and fluid mechanics.

## What is Classical Mechanics?

Classical mechanics is the branch of physics that deals with the motion of objects under the influence of forces. It includes Newton's laws of motion, conservation of energy and momentum, and the study of mechanical systems.

## Topics Covered

- Motion and kinematics
- Forces and dynamics
- Gravity and orbital motion
- Mechanical vibrations and waves
- Rotational motion
- Fluid mechanics
